# Daily Rituals & Journal

## Overview
Polaris provides dedicated morning and evening bookends to establish daily momentum and preserve personal reflections. The rituals engine rewards consistency, while the journal and mood tracker provide long-term emotional and cognitive patterns.

## Core Systems

### 1. Daily Rituals Engine
- **Three Ritual Cadences**:
  - **Morning Rituals**: Grounding actions before deep work (hydration, movement, reviewing priorities).
  - **Anytime Rituals**: Flexible mid-day habits (protein targets, hydration, reading).
  - **Evening Rituals**: Wind-down and shutdown routines (reflection, clearing backlog, planning tomorrow).
- **Streak & XP Rewards**:
  - Checking off a ritual grants immediate XP.
  - Consistent daily completion increments habit streaks and unlocks periodic sweep awards.

### 2. Journal & Reflections
- **Daily Reflection Prompts**: Prompts for gratitude, wins, lessons learned, and friction points.
- **Supabase Cloud Sync**: Encrypted, synchronized entry storage accessible across devices.
- **Offline Entry Support**: Write reflections locally in Dexie cache with automatic background sync when reconnected.

### 3. Year in Pixels (Mood Tracker)
- **Compact Color Matrix**: Visualizes every day of the calendar year as an interactive micro-square.
- **Mood Scale**: Quick 5-tier mood rating that displays annual emotional trends at a single glance.
