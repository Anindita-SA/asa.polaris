# Fitness Bridge

## Overview
The Fitness Bridge centralizes physical conditioning, strength training, nutrition logging, and body composition analytics into Polaris. It connects physical health directly to character vitality and leveling XP.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Workout Logging & Exercise Sets
- **Workout Sessions**:
  - Track structured strength sessions, resistance routines, and cardio movement.
  - Record discrete exercise sets with weight, repetitions, and perceived exertion (RPE).
- **Template Routines**:
  - Pre-configure recurring exercise templates to start a session in seconds.
  - Track progressive overload week over week.

### 2. Nutrition & Macro Tracking
- **Meal Logging**:
  - Track daily meals across Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, and Dinner.
  - Record protein, carbohydrate, and fat metrics alongside estimated meal costs.
- **Calibrated Macro Estimates**:
  - Built-in sanity guards prevent inflated AI macro estimations, calibrating realistic protein amounts.
- **Daily Target Progress**:
  - High-contrast visual gauges monitor daily protein and caloric targets in real time.

### 3. Body Metrics & Trend Analytics
- **Weight & Measurement Logs**:
  - Log morning weigh-ins and waist measurements.
  - Rolling 7-day and 14-day moving averages smooth out daily water weight fluctuations.
- **Heartfelt AI Mentor Integration**:
  - Edge function analysis that reviews 14-day training consistency and nutrition logs, delivering validating praise and actionable guidance.
