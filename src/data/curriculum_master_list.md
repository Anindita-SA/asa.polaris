## 4. Intellectual Omnivore Track (Accent: `#EC4899`)

*Goal: Investigation, writing, law, and philosophy literacy alongside masters prep. A parallel track where history and philosophy are passive reading, while writing and journalism require active practice.*

### Sequencing & Platform Strategy
- **Phase 1**: Start with Knight Center (structured, project-based, closest to the missing muscle).
- **Phase 2**: Add writing practice with real feedback in parallel.
- **Phase 3**: Law and philosophy stay passive, pick up only once Phase 1 and 2 have momentum.
- **Platform Rules**: Coursera audit can lock content if unfinished. edX audit sometimes expires (4-6mo). YouTube/OCW never expires or locks, use for anything low-priority/no-deadline.

### Journalism & Investigation (Active)
*Active skill-building requiring feedback and output.*
- **[NEXT]** [Knight Center for Journalism in the Americas](https://journalismcourses.org/) - free, self-paced, data journalism + investigative reporting, real certs
- [GIJN Resource Center](https://gijn.org/resource/) - free guides, asynchronous

### Writing (Active)
*Actual peer critique is non-negotiable since courses alone don't build this muscle.*
- [Coursera audit: "Good with Words: Writing and Editing"](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/good-with-words) (University of Michigan)
- [Scribophile](https://www.scribophile.com/) or [r/DestructiveReaders](https://www.reddit.com/r/DestructiveReaders/) - real peer critique

### Law (Passive)
*Passive absorption with no deadline pressure.*
- [Yale Open Courses (Law)](https://oyc.yale.edu/) / [MIT OCW (Law)](https://ocw.mit.edu/) - intro law or contracts lectures

### Philosophy (Passive)
*Passive absorption with no deadline pressure.*
- [Oxford Philosophy Podcasts](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/series/philosophy-beginners)
- [Nigel Warburton, "Philosophy: The Classics"](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31899.Philosophy) as reading companion
