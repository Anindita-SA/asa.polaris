Polaris Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 13, 2026

1. Overview and Scope

Polaris is an offline-first personal productivity Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed and developed by Anindita Sarker Aloka. Polaris functions as an intelligent personal command center providing task prioritization (Eisenhower Matrix, WSJF scoring), habit tracking, curriculum logging, life domain mapping, and focus management.

This Privacy Policy explains how Polaris collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access or use the application at https://anindita-sa.github.io/asa.polaris/ or through any associated services and repositories.

2. Information We Collect

Polaris collects only the minimum amount of data required to provide its productivity features:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your basic profile details, including your name, email address, and profile picture URL.
  • Google Calendar & Tasks Data: If you explicitly authorize calendar and tasks integration, Polaris accesses your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks via authorized Google OAuth scopes to display schedules and synchronize commitments inside your personal timeline.
  • User-Generated Productivity Data: Tasks, subtasks, goals, habit entries, habit logs, curriculum notes, milestones, Eisenhower matrix coordinates, and journal reflections created within the application.
  • Local Device Preferences: User interface preferences, notification mode settings, and local offline cache data stored in browser IndexedDB and LocalStorage.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by Polaris is used exclusively for personal productivity and self-management functions:

  • Authentication and Account Security: Authenticating your session and verifying identity via Supabase Auth and Google OAuth.
  • Task Prioritization and Management: Calculating Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) scores, organizing tasks across Eisenhower matrix quadrants, and planning daily execution.
  • Calendar and Schedule Visibility: Rendering upcoming events, study blocks, and task deadlines within your private timeline.
  • Habit Tracking and Progress: Tracking streaks, habit compliance, and leveling metrics for personal development.
  • Offline Synchronization: Caching records locally in browser IndexedDB (via Dexie.js) to enable seamless offline work and bidirectional synchronization when online.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Polaris requests access to Google user data strictly to enable direct user-facing productivity features within the application.

Google Limited Use Compliance Statement:
Polaris's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Strict Restrictions on Google User Data:

  • No Advertising or Marketing: We do not use, share, or sell Google user data for advertising, retargeting, personalized recommendations, or market research purposes.
  • No Unauthorized Transfers: We do not transfer Google user data to third parties unless required to provide or improve core productivity features explicitly requested by the user, or as required by applicable law.
  • Human Review Restrictions: Humans do not read Google user data unless:
    • You have provided explicit permission to inspect specific data for troubleshooting or technical support;
    • It is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating a bug or security incident); or
    • It is required to comply with applicable laws and legal processes.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models without explicit user consent.

5. Data Storage, Isolation, and Security

We employ industry-standard architectural safeguards to protect your data:

  • Database Isolation: Data is stored in PostgreSQL hosted on Supabase. Every query is strictly isolated using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies scoped directly to the authenticated user ID.
  • Local Storage: Local offline records are stored in your browser's IndexedDB via Dexie.js and are accessible only by your local browser instance.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between your client browser, Supabase servers, and Google APIs occurs exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections using TLS 1.3.

6. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

You maintain full ownership and control over all data stored in Polaris:

  • Instant JSON Export: You can export a complete snapshot of all your tasks, goals, habits, milestones, and profile data in standard JSON format at any time directly from the in-app Settings panel.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account, database records, and associated data at any time by emailing anindita.polaris@gmail.com. Upon receipt of your request, all personal records associated with your account will be permanently expunged from the database.

7. Third-Party Services

Polaris integrates with the following trusted third-party service providers:

  • Supabase: Provides backend authentication, PostgreSQL database storage, and edge computing infrastructure. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google APIs & OAuth: Provides identity authentication, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks connectivity. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Polaris does not use tracking cookies or third-party marketing cookies. Local storage and IndexedDB are used exclusively for:

  • Persisting active authentication session tokens so you remain signed in.
  • Saving local UI display preferences (such as canvas filters and notification settings).
  • Offline caching of your productivity records to enable offline app functionality.
