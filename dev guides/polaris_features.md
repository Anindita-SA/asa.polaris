# Polaris Master Features Catalog & Curriculum Roadmap

Polaris is a **Premium Life Guide & Self-Evolution Dashboard** engineered specifically to merge spatial alignment with tactical, ADHD-friendly daily execution. This catalog documents all distinct, high-end features and details the current vs. projected state of the Personal Curriculum.

---

## 1. The Personal Curriculum (Current vs. Projected)

The Curriculum is your structured self-education hub. It is where you organize what you are studying or consuming to drive your core identity nodes.

### ── Current State (The Foundation)
1. **Academic/Career/Self Partition:** Chapters and topics are partitioned under three master node tabs.
2. **Standard Task Lists:** Topics function as flat checkable lists within an expandable chapter container.
3. **Completion Progress:** Complete topics award standard XP and display a basic numeric fraction progress indicator.

### ── Projected State (The Life Navigator Overhaul 🚀)
1. **The Media & Watchlist Integration:** A dedicated golden **Media & Reading** tab added. Seeded with `Books`, `Movies`, and `Shows` to track completed dates, personal context notes, and star ratings (`⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐`).
2. **Drag-and-Drop Reordering:** Fluid rearranging of chapters and topics.
3. **Curriculum-Goal Integration:** Click any curriculum topic to convert it directly into an active Daily/Weekly goal.

## 2. Definitive Features Catalog

The following is the complete inventory of distinct premium features currently designed for implementation:

### Component 1: Endless Leveling Engine & 200-Level Curve
* **Formula:** $\text{XP} = 15 \times L^2$ (Level 1 to 200).
* **20 Dynamic Rank Titles:** Dynamic ranks and grades (e.g., `Circuit Weaver, Grade 8`).

### Component 2: Daily Repeating Task Loop
* **Auto-Cloning Forward:** Automatically carries active previous repeating tasks forward as fresh, unchecked items when a new day loads.

### Component 3: Campaign Goals vs. Side Quests
* **The Main Campaign:** Hierarchical, parent-linked academic/career goals.
* **Side Quests Guild:** Standalone curiosity-driven targets with unique **Compass Badges**.

### Component 4: Spatial Drill-Down Sync
* **Camera Focus:** Clicking any subnode star on the Map filters **Goals** and **Timeline** lists below instantly.

### Component 5: "Whispering" Context Layers
* **Expandable Drawer:** Smooth glassmorphic accordion reveals personal **Why-Now Context or Guiding Quotes** without screen clutter.

### Component 6: Seamless Goal Editing
* **Pen (Edit) Modal:** Elegantly update Goal Title, Targets, Deadlines, Parent Links, and Rewards on the fly.

### Component 7: Unified Goal-Timeline Map
* **Dated Roadmap:** Renders master milestones and dated Goals chronologically on one continuous, glowing flight path.

### Component 8: Fixed AI Timeline Step Generator
* **Schema Fix:** Resolves JSON conflicts guaranteeing 100% successful, ADHD-friendly generation of micro-steps.

### Component 9: AI Goal Alignment Auditor
* **Star-Map Hybrid Prompt:** Combines SMART and HARD models to deliver heartfelt validation followed by scannable, constructive strategy critique.

### Component 10: Google Calendar Sync & Workload Estimator
* **Sync Button:** Pre-populates GCal URLs with titles, estimated durations, and descriptions for instant block-scheduling.

### Component 11: Integrated Pomodoro Focus Soundtracks
* **Footer Audio Deck:** Ambient track selections (Lofi, White Noise) integrated inside the timer footer with auto-pause.

### Component 12: Tactile I/O System (Ground-Up Overhaul)
* **Accrued Logging:** A tactile "Log Accrued Minutes" button to save early stops instantly.

### Component 13: Media Reading & Watch List
* **Curriculum Journaling:** Log Dates, Notes, and Star Ratings for Books, Movies, and Shows directly inside your dashboard.

### Component 14: [COMPLETED] One-Click Guest Demo Mode

### Component 15: Interactive Stats Nav & Insights Modal
